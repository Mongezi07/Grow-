# Vehicle Quote Marketplace Business Blueprint

## 1) Business concept
Create a **digital marketplace for vehicle quotations** where buyers submit one profile and receive competing offers from verified dealership sales agents—without pressure calls.

### Core value proposition
- **For buyers:** Compare multiple vehicle finance/cash quotes quickly and privately.
- **For dealerships:** Access high-intent, pre-profiled leads at lower acquisition cost.
- **For the market:** More transparency, less friction, faster time to quote.

---

## 2) Problem and solution fit

### Current problems buyers face
- Repeating personal and financial details at many dealerships.
- Unwanted follow-up calls and pressure selling.
- Difficult side-by-side quote comparisons.
- Limited transparency on fees and financing terms.

### Proposed solution
A platform where users:
1. Create a profile once.
2. Upload required documents securely.
3. Select desired vehicles.
4. Receive structured quotes from multiple agents.
5. Choose to engage only with preferred offers.

**Communication policy:** In-app messaging by default, phone contact only with explicit buyer consent.

---

## 3) Target users

### Primary users
- Car buyers (new and used) who want convenience and price transparency.

### Secondary users
- Dealership sales agents and finance teams.
- Dealership managers who need measurable lead conversion.

---

## 4) Platform workflow

1. **Buyer onboarding**
   - Create account.
   - Complete profile (identity, budget, location, employment/income optional based on quote type).
2. **Document submission**
   - Upload IDs, proof of income, proof of address, etc.
   - Automated document checks + manual verification queue.
3. **Vehicle intent creation**
   - Select make/model/year/trim preferences.
   - Choose purchase mode (cash, financed, lease).
4. **Quote request routing**
   - System distributes request to matched verified dealerships.
5. **Dealership quote submission**
   - Agents submit standard quote template (vehicle price, fees, APR/rate, deposit, monthly installment, validity).
6. **Buyer comparison and shortlist**
   - Buyer compares offers in a normalized format.
   - Buyer can accept, reject, ask questions, or request revised offer.
7. **Opt-in contact + deal closure**
   - Contact details unlocked only when buyer opts in.

---

## 5) Trust, safety, and compliance

- **Consent-first communication controls** (no unsolicited calls).
- **Role-based access control** for dealership staff.
- **KYC/identity verification** for buyers where needed.
- **Audit logs** for quote activity and data access.
- **Data protection compliance:** POPIA/GDPR-style principles (consent, minimization, retention policies, right to delete/export).
- **Document encryption** at rest and in transit.

---

## 6) Revenue model

### Option A: Lead fee
- Dealership pays per qualified lead (e.g., verified profile + active vehicle intent).

### Option B: Success fee
- Dealership pays when a deal is marked won.

### Option C: Subscription tiers
- Basic: limited monthly quote responses.
- Pro: unlimited responses + analytics + priority placement.

### Option D: Value-added products
- Featured dealer placements.
- Finance partner referrals.
- Vehicle insurance referral commissions.

---

## 7) MVP scope (first 8–12 weeks)

### Must-have features
- Buyer registration + profile management.
- Secure document upload.
- Vehicle request form.
- Dealership portal for quote submission.
- Standardized quote comparison dashboard.
- In-app messaging.
- Consent gate before phone/email disclosure.
- Admin verification dashboard.

### Nice-to-have (post-MVP)
- AI-based quote ranking/recommendations.
- Credit pre-qualification integrations.
- WhatsApp/SMS alerts (opt-in).
- Trade-in valuation module.

---

## 8) Go-to-market strategy

1. **Pilot city launch** with 10–20 dealerships.
2. Focus on **high-demand vehicle categories** first.
3. Offer early partner incentives (reduced fees first 3 months).
4. Run buyer acquisition via:
   - Social ads targeting in-market shoppers.
   - SEO landing pages for make/model quote searches.
   - Partnerships with insurance/finance brokers.
5. Publish trust signals:
   - "No pressure calls" pledge.
   - Verified dealership badges.
   - Transparent quote breakdowns.

---

## 9) Core KPIs

### Buyer metrics
- Time to first quote.
- Quotes per request.
- Request-to-deal conversion.
- User satisfaction/NPS.
- Opt-in rate for direct contact.

### Dealership metrics
- Response time.
- Win rate per quote.
- Cost per acquired customer.
- Churn rate by dealership.

### Platform metrics
- Monthly active buyers.
- Monthly active dealership agents.
- Gross merchandise value (GMV) influenced.
- Revenue per dealership.

---

## 10) Risks and mitigation

- **Low dealer participation:** Start with incentives, account management, and clear ROI reporting.
- **Quote quality inconsistency:** Enforce standardized quote schema + validation rules.
- **Spam/over-contacting buyers:** Strict communication permissions + penalties.
- **Data/privacy incidents:** Strong security controls, penetration testing, incident response plan.

---

## 11) Suggested next actions

1. Validate demand with 20 buyer interviews and 10 dealership interviews.
2. Build low-fidelity prototype (buyer flow + dealer quote form).
3. Define legal/compliance requirements for your target country.
4. Launch concierge MVP (manual operations behind a lightweight web app).
5. Measure conversion funnel and iterate before scaling.
